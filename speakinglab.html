THE LANGUAGE.

USE IT?

  • You understand English when you read docs or listen to calls.

02 / The Concept

LESS TEACHING.
MORE SPEAKING.

You don't need another explanation of English. You need to use the English you already know.

  • Live practice instead of lectures.
  • Real discussions instead of textbook exercises.
  • Spontaneous speech instead of scripted answers.
  • Work on what you actually say — not abstract mistakes.

07 / FAQ

Practical questions.

Who is Speaking Lab for?

For IT specialists who already understand English, but want to use it freely in live conversation.

What English level do I need?

You need to hold a basic conversation in English. Not designed for learning from scratch.

Is this a conversation club?

No. Sessions are actively moderated by a Host around debates, negotiations, and professional tasks.

Will I study grammar?

No traditional grammar lessons. Feedback is based directly on your real speech.

How much speaking time will I get?

Maximum 4 participants per cohort. Active participation is required from everyone.

What happens after the programme?

The cohort finishes on schedule. You leave with intensive speaking mileage, feedback, and habits you can apply independently.
