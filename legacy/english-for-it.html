07 / Practical Details

What English level do I need?

A solid pre-intermediate to intermediate base (B1+) so you can participate in technical discussions. We determine your exact starting point during initial diagnostics.

Is this a programming course?

No. We assume you already have the engineering knowledge. Our job is to help you articulate, structure, and defend that knowledge in English.

Are mock interviews included?

Yes. Realistic mock interviews with detailed structural and language feedback are a core part of the program.

Do I need to choose a technical track?

Yes, we align discussions and mock scenarios with your primary domain — Frontend, Backend, ML, or Product/QA.

How is my progress evaluated?

Through recurring mock interviews, recorded self-presentations, and targeted feedback on clarity, structure, and argumentation.
