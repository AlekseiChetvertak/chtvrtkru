07 / FAQ

Practical questions.

Who is Speaking Lab for?

For IT specialists and tech professionals who already understand English, but want to use it freely and persuasively in live conversation.

What English level do I need?

You need to be able to hold a basic conversation in English. This programme is not designed for learning from scratch.

Is this a conversation club?

No. Sessions are actively structured and moderated by a Host around debates, negotiations, and real professional scenarios.

Will I study grammar?

There are no traditional grammar lectures. All feedback is derived directly from what you actually say during sessions.

How much speaking time will I get?

With a strict cap of 4 participants per cohort, there is nowhere to hide. Every session requires your active participation.

What happens after the programme?

The cohort concludes on schedule. You leave with intensive speaking mileage, targeted feedback, and communication habits you can apply independently.
